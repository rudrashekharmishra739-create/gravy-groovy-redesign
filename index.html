Our Story

A warm hug on every plate

Gravy Groovy was created with one simple goal — to serve food that feels like a warm hug. We capture the essence of Indian comfort cooking and present it in a vibrant, contemporary setting.

From slow-cooked gravies and fragrant spices to thoughtfully chosen ingredients, every dish is a celebration of regional cuisines from across India. Great food tastes even better when shared with the people you love.
