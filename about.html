About Us

The Story of Gravy Groovy

Created with a simple goal — to serve food that feels like a warm hug.

Our Beginnings

Born in Lucknow, built on comfort

The founders wanted to capture the essence of Indian comfort cooking while presenting it in a vibrant, contemporary setting in the heart of Lucknow.

Every detail — from the menu to the ambience — is crafted to make guests feel relaxed, welcome and well taken care of. We believe great food tastes even better when shared with the people you love.

Food Philosophy

Every recipe is a living story

Traditional techniques are respected, regional influences celebrated, and new ideas welcomed into the kitchen. The result is a menu that feels familiar yet constantly surprising.

We focus on freshness, balance and consistency. Slow-cooked gravies, fragrant spices and thoughtfully chosen ingredients come together to create plates that are rich, colourful and deeply satisfying.

The Experience

Relaxed, cheerful, memorable

From the moment you step through the door, our team aims to create a relaxed, cheerful atmosphere. Whether a quick lunch, family dinner or celebration with friends, we want you comfortable staying as long as you like.

Soft lighting, friendly service and a playlist that complements the mood turn an ordinary meal into a memorable experience.

Our People

Meet the people behind the plates

Our kitchen and service teams share one belief: food is a way of caring for people. Many chefs grew up cooking family recipes; others bring professional experience from across hospitality.

Together they experiment, refine and perfect dishes before they reach your table. The same attention to detail continues on the floor, where every server and host looks for ways to make your visit smoother and more enjoyable.

We constantly evolve, listen to feedback and look for new ways to surprise guests — while staying true to the comforting soul of Indian home-style cooking.

Catering & Events

Celebrate with us

Weddings, birthdays, corporate lunches or intimate gatherings — we craft custom menus and handle the details so you can enjoy the moment.
